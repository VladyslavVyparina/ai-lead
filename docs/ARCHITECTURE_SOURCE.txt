1. Scope, decisions and boundaries
1.1 Mission and MVP outcome
Build a scalable, configurable workflow that converts discovered or inbound businesses into verified leads, working personalised preview websites, approved proposals, controlled outreach and measurable sales outcomes. The result must be maintainable and improvable by the owner without routine developer involvement.

3.4 Reliability rules
• Every transition writes an immutable event with actor, reason, version, correlation ID and idempotency key.
• One provider/city SourceRun can fail and resume from checkpoint without blocking other runs or creating duplicates.
• Suppression overrides retries, follow-ups and campaign schedules. Revoked approval invalidates queued messages.
• Every generated fact and price links to evidence/Pricebook version; low-confidence output routes to review.

7.3 Outreach principles
• Use a named human sender and European Portuguese by default. Lead with one verified business observation, one outcome and a permission-based CTA; keep AI implementation secondary.
• One lead timeline: Day 1 email/form; Day 3-4 social follow-up; Day 7 useful insight/preview; Day 12 close. Reply, opt-out, bounce or pause stops the sequence.
• WhatsApp is for invited, opted-in or prospect-initiated business conversations. No scraped-list broadcasting, fake scarcity, invented proof or promised uplift.
• Positive or ambiguous replies pause automation and transfer to a human for qualification, meeting and proposal.
7.4 Experiment plan
Primary P0 objective: at least five qualified conversations from 30 approved contacts. Change one major variable at a time and decide weekly from qualified conversations, meetings, proposals, wins, opt-outs, CAC and cost per SQL, not opens or clicks.
GTM LOCK
Use European Portuguese by default, a named human sender and one coordinated timeline. WhatsApp follows invitation/opt-in; every reply, opt-out, bounce or manual pause cancels remaining touches.

9.1 Definition of done
• Owner can add/pause a city or source instance, change scoring, import/publish a template, update Pricebook/proposal/message configuration and rollback without core code changes.
• The same pipeline processes discovered, CSV/manual and generic inbound leads without source-specific workflow forks.
• Fifteen Tier A working websites and fifteen Tier B packages pass factual, functional, brand and commercial QA and cover all pilot cities/source routes.
• Every Tier A URL loads successfully; navigation/contact links work; concept form/booking requests route safely; deployments can expire or be removed; client routing activates only after sale and approval.
• A routine per-lead build changes SiteConfig/JSON Patch only; schema validation, build, functional tests and deployment run through reusable CLI/MCP adapters without a full-repository LLM rewrite.
• Every task records model/tool/version, tokens, fees, latency and QA result; campaign and per-lead budgets stop or downgrade work before overspend.
• No send bypasses approval, consent/channel or suppression; webhook and job retries are idempotent.
• Tests cover dedupe, routing, scoring, source failure/resume, template validation, unsupported facts/prices, revoked approval, opt-out and 1,000-record load.
• Handoff includes setup, data dictionary, config guide, adapter/template contracts, operating runbook, risks, demo video and owner walkthrough.
FINAL GATE
Ship only when the owner can operate and improve the platform, the 30-package cohort is factually correct, the first-GTM decisions are configured, and outreach is measurable without exposing credentials or personal data.
